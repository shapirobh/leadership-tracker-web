Leadership Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Leadership Tracker has no backend server and does not transmit your data to us or any third party.

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored in your own Google Drive folder. The app reads and writes files solely within the folder you select during setup. We use the drive.file scope, which limits the app's access to only the files and folders it creates or that you explicitly grant it access to.

Authentication

Leadership Tracker uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate you. Your refresh token is stored securely on your device using the Android Keystore (via Expo SecureStore). We never see, store, or transmit your Google credentials.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. All information remains in your Google Drive under your control.

Shared Folders

Leadership Tracker is designed for use with a shared Google Drive folder among a small leadership group (e.g., a bishopric or stake presidency). Important: Share your Leadership Tracker folder only with named Google accounts of authorized members. Never set the folder to "anyone with the link," as this could expose sensitive member information to unintended parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete any data at any time through the app or directly in Google Drive. Deleted records go to Google Drive's Trash and are permanently removed after 30 days. You can also revoke the app's access at any time through your Google Account settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
