Leadership Tracker

Only share this folder with the named Google accounts of your leadership team — never "Anyone with the link."
Why one file at a time? To keep your information private, Google only lets an app open files you pick yourself. So you grant this app access to each of your unit's files once, right here — a one-time setup that takes about a minute, and you won't have to do it again. Tap Select next to a file, choose it, and a green check appears. If two files share a name, pick the one inside the folder you selected.
