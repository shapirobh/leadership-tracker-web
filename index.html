Leadership Tracker

Organize callings, interviews, and meeting agendas — together, in one place.

Leadership Tracker is a mobile app for local volunteer leadership groups — such as a ward bishopric or stake presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints — to coordinate the work they share. It runs on Android (iOS coming later) and is currently in pre-release testing ahead of its Google Play launch.

What the app does

  • Callings — track each calling through its full workflow: leadership approval, the invitation interview, sustaining, setting apart, and the record update, with reminders when a step has waited too long.
  • Interviews — schedule recurring interviews (with rotation between leaders) and one-time appointments, see everything due on a single worklist, and book times against your team's shared calendar so double-booking is visible before it happens.
  • Meeting agendas — build sacrament and leadership meeting programs: speakers and topics, prayers, hymns, business items, and a printable agenda PDF.
  • Built for a team — every member of the leadership group sees the same up-to-date information on their own phone, in their own language (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French).

Your data stays yours

Leadership Tracker has no backend server and collects nothing. Everything you enter is stored on your device — or, if you choose shared mode, in a Google Drive folder that you create and own, shared only with the people you invite. The app connects to your Google account for exactly two purposes:

  • Google Drive — the app stores its data files in the one folder you select. It uses Google's limited drive.file permission, which means it cannot see, touch, or list anything else in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar — if your team links a calendar, the app writes interview appointments to it and reads it so you can see open time slots while scheduling. It only ever touches the one calendar you select.

No data is ever transmitted to the developer or any third party. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings, and your files remain yours in your own Drive. The app also works fully in a device-only mode with no Google account at all.
